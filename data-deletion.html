Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This page explains how users of the Tuzzina application can delete the data that Tuzzina stores about them or about their connected social accounts (for example, Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts).

What data Tuzzina stores

  • Access tokens issued by the platforms you connect;
  • Connected account details (page or account name and identifier);
  • Posts, schedules and media that you create in Tuzzina;
  • Basic analytics data related to the accounts you manage.

How to delete your data

Option 1 — From inside Tuzzina

Disconnect the platform account from within the application (remove the connected channel). This immediately deletes the stored access tokens for that account.

Option 2 — Revoke access from the platform

You can also remove Tuzzina's access from the platform itself, for example on Facebook: Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → find the Tuzzina app → Remove. This stops any future access; to also delete data already stored, use Option 3.

Option 3 — Email request

Send an email to donbayamusic@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and specify the connected account(s) concerned. We will delete the associated stored data within 30 days and confirm completion by email.

What happens after deletion

Once deleted, the data cannot be recovered, and any pending scheduled posts associated with the deleted account will be cancelled. Deleting data in Tuzzina does not delete content that was already published on the social platforms themselves — those must be deleted from the platform directly.

Contact

For any question about data deletion: donbayamusic@gmail.com.
