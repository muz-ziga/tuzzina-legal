Tuzzina

A private project for analyzing data within my own platforms and promoting them through posts on Facebook and Instagram.

About

I am an independent web developer and the founder of the Juzzir and Muzziga platforms — global platforms that belong to my own businesses. Tuzzina is a new project of mine: it focuses on analyzing the data inside my platforms and on promoting my products through posts published to Facebook and Instagram. It is built with modern technology and contemporary algorithms, with the goal of presenting my products in a professional, secure and world-class way.

Tuzzina serves me alone. I am its owner, its administrator and its only user: I manage and monitor everything myself. Tuzzina does not collect any data, and it has no other users. I operate strictly according to the terms of every platform I connect to — for example, I follow Meta's terms when working with Facebook and Instagram, and the terms of each other platform I use. I do not collect any data through these platforms; all of the data I work with comes from my own platforms, which I founded and which are completely independent.
