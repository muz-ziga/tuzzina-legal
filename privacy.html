Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Tuzzina is a self-hosted social media scheduling application, operated privately by its owner ("we", "us"). It is used to plan, schedule, publish and analyze content on the social media pages and channels that we own and manage, including Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what data the application accesses, how it is used, and how it can be deleted.

1. Data we access

When an account (for example, a Facebook Page) is connected to Tuzzina, we receive access tokens for that account and, through the official platform APIs (such as the Meta Graph API), we can access only what the granted permissions allow, for example:

  • Page or account information (name, identifier);
  • Posts, media and comments that we publish or manage;
  • Basic engagement insights for the pages and accounts we manage.

2. How we use the data

  • To schedule, publish and delete posts on the pages and channels we manage;
  • To display analytics and reporting inside Tuzzina;
  • To operate, maintain and secure the application.

We do not sell, rent or trade any data, and we do not use it for advertising purposes.

3. Storage and security

All data is stored on our own private servers. Access tokens and credentials are kept confidential, are never exposed publicly, and are used only to operate the functionality described above. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect the data we store.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Connected accounts can be disconnected at any time from within Tuzzina, which immediately removes the stored access tokens for that account.
  • You can also revoke access from the platform itself (for example, in your Facebook security settings).
  • To request deletion of any data stored by Tuzzina, contact donbayamusic@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

5. Third-party services

Tuzzina communicates with the platforms whose accounts you connect (for example, Meta / Facebook) using their official APIs. The processing of data by those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with the updated date shown above.

7. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data: donbayamusic@gmail.com.
